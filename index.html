About

I build backend systems that other teams depend on.

I'm a backend engineer with roughly six years building and operating services at scale. Most of that was at Walmart Global Tech, on the platform external third-party sellers use to manage their business — a system where a bad deploy is somebody's livelihood, so correctness and careful rollout mattered more than speed.

I'm drawn to the parts of the job that don't demo well: decomposing a monolith without anyone noticing, making event-driven consumers idempotent, designing an API that won't need breaking changes in a year. I work in Java, Spring Boot and Kafka, and I've shipped net-new systems as well as migrated old ones.

I'm based near Seattle and completed an M.S. in Computer Science at UT Arlington in 2026. I'm currently looking for backend engineering roles.
